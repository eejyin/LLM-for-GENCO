    03 / MODEL EXPLORER

    How does GENCO learn?

    PowerFlow

    A heterogeneous grid graph is projected and refined repeatedly. Each stage decodes electrical states, evaluates power-balance residuals, and feeds them into the next stage. Supervised and physics losses train the learnable weights.

    ↶ Feedback to next refinement

    Physics-loss weight by refinement stage

    Local implementation: wᵢ = λ^(N−i) / Σ λ^(N−k), then multiply by the physics-loss mixing coefficient. These bars are computed from configuration, not measured training losses.
